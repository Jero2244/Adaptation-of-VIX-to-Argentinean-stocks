# Rights ex-date check, 2016-01 to 2018-04

Written 2026-11-04. An independent agent wrote this text from public sources. It read no project file. The main session saved its returned report here without a change to the findings. All prices are removed. The agent saw prices while it worked (section 5).

## 1. Verdict

"Yes, rights existed but ADS ex-date not found."

- A preferential rights offering took place in September 2017. It gave preemptive and accretion rights to existing shareholders.
- The final prospectus supplement says these rights were not made available to ADS holders. On that text, the ADS had no direct right to lose, so a true ADS rights ex-date may not exist.
- I found no US ex-date in any source.

Confidence:
- High: the rights existed, and ADS holders could not take part.
- Medium: no ADS ex-date or depositary event exists. I could not read the Nasdaq or BNY Mellon records.

These items would change the verdict:
- A BNY Mellon notice that sets an ADS rights record date, or that reports a sale of the rights with net proceeds paid to holders.
- A Nasdaq corporate action notice with an ex-date for the ADS.

## 2. Offering facts

Approvals and registration:
- Board meeting and CNV: the shareholders' meeting of 2017-08-15 approved up to 150,000,000 new Class B shares. It also cut the rights exercise term to the legal minimum of 10 days.
- CNV approved the public offering on 2017-09-07. BYMA approved the listing on 2017-09-11.
- The F-3ASR and the preliminary 424B5 were filed on 2017-09-08. The final 424B2 was filed on 2017-09-27.

Dates:
- Preferential period end: 2017-09-26. The preliminary supplement says "on or about" that date. The final supplement says it "expired" on that date.
- Preferential period start: not found. A 10-day term implies a start in mid-September 2017, but this is my inference.
- Record date: not found. The two prospectus supplements give none in the parts that I read.
- Pricing announcement: 2017-09-26. ADS delivery: 2017-09-29.

Rights ratio:
- No filing that I read states the ratio.
- My derivation gives about 0.10031 new shares per share held, or 1 per 9.97. It is not a quoted figure.
- Calculation: 130,434,600 (preliminary maximum) / 1,300,264,597 (shares outstanding). Applied to EBA Holding's 281,221,650 Class A shares, this gives 28,210,438. The underwriting agreement states 28,210,437, so the figures agree within one share.

ADS holders:
- They received no rights in this offering. The rights were not registered under the Securities Act.
- Tradability of the rights for ADS holders: not applicable, because the rights were not made available to them.
- The filing says that rights go "to all holders of our ordinary shares (including to the Depositary)". I found no text on what happened to the rights that attached to the depositary's shares.
- The text on a depositary sale of rights refers to future offerings. It does not report a sale in 2017.

Share counts:
- Base offering: 109,999,996 Class B shares.
- Preferential subscription: 17,674,423 shares, or 16.07% of the offering. Argentine placement: 3,001,653 shares.
- Underwriters' option: 14,976,080 shares (1,497,608 ADSs). A further 1,523,924 shares went to Argentine investors through preferential rights.
- The additional shares total 16,500,004.
- Outstanding shares: 1,300,264,597 before, 1,410,264,593 at 2017-09-30, and 1,426,764,597 in a 2018 interim filing. The arithmetic agrees.
- EBA Holding transferred its rights to Galicia Valores S.A. Galicia Valores could exercise them at the direction of the underwriters, and the resulting shares could be delivered as ADSs.

Source conflicts:
- The preliminary size was 130,434,600 shares. The priced size was 110 million plus an option of up to 16.5 million. The difference comes from the stage of the offering.
- One search summary described the rights as held by "Argentine shareholders", and so does the company's press release. The prospectus text says "existing shareholders", with US holders and ADS holders excluded.
- Warning: the "1 per 89.54" ratio in web results belongs to the January 2025 offering. Do not apply it to 2017.

- ADS split: none found.
- Stock dividend: none found. The dividends found were cash.
  - Fiscal 2015: Ps.150 million, approved 2016-04-26.
  - Fiscal 2016: Ps.240 million, available from 2017-05-09.

I saw no distribution to holders, but I did not read the full terms.
- 2017-09-26 item: I found no distribution near that date.
  - The issuer filed no 6-K between 2017-09-08 and 2017-09-27. The 6-K of 2017-09-27 and the 6-K of 2017-10-02 concern the offering.
  - The only 2017 dividend notice found is the May notice.
  - 2017-09-26 is also the end of the preferential period and the pricing date. This is a fact about the dates. No source links the vendor item to the offering.
  - I cannot exclude a depositary payment of rights-sale proceeds. I found no evidence for one.
